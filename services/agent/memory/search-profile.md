## Writing rules for applications
- English by default; Russian for Russian-language vacancies.
- Only facts from cv.md, with its numbers. No invented tools, years or results.
- Short: cover letter 120-180 words, 3 short paragraphs: why this role, 2-3 matching results with numbers, call to action with the site link and the intro-call link.
- No clichés ("passionate", "dynamic team", "I am writing to apply"), no emoji.
- Mention the CKA preparation only if the vacancy asks for Kubernetes certification.
